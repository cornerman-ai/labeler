Footwork steps — how it works

Every video in the list has one 20-second window, the same for everyone. Label every step in it, one foot at a time: watch the window for the lead foot (the front one) and mark each of its steps, then say the foot is done; then the same for the rear foot. A step runs from the frame the foot leaves the floor to the frame it lands; its direction is the way the foot moved, in the boxer's own front / back / left / right — facing the camera, his left is on your right. The centre key is a pivot: the foot turns on the ball without moving to a new spot. A bounce in place is not a step. "Done" counts even when the foot never stepped — that is a window with zero steps, not an unlabeled one.

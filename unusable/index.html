Unusable footage — how it works

Pick a video, watch it with the skeleton on, and mark every stretch where the skeleton is not the boxer's or is not there: set Start and End at the playhead, then the reason (1–5). When the whole video is done, press R for reviewed, and the list moves on to the next unreviewed video.

The detected lane under the skeleton lane is what the skeleton files say by themselves: no skeleton for 3 frames or more, the skeleton jumps — more than a torso within a quarter second, usually onto someone else. Hints to check on the footage, not labels; click one to go there.

Spans go to the Unusable Spans tab, review marks to Unusable Reviewed, both in the labels workbook. "Whole video unusable" moves the video's labeling rows to Skeleton Problems.

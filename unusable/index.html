Unusable footage — how it works

Watch the video with the skeleton on and mark every stretch where the skeleton is not the boxer's or is not there: Enter at the start, Enter at the end, then the reason — the span saves itself. When the reason changes mid-stretch (the boxer walks out, then the tracker lands on someone else), make two spans back to back, one per reason: the end of the first is the start of the second. When the whole video is done, press R for reviewed and the list moves on to the next unreviewed video.

The detected lane

Under the skeleton lane, what the skeleton files say by themselves: no skeleton for 3 frames or more, the skeleton jumps — more than a torso within a quarter second, usually onto someone or something else. Hints to check on the footage, not labels; click one to go there.

Spans go to the Unusable Spans tab, review marks to Unusable Reviewed, both in the labels workbook. "Whole video unusable" moves the video's labeling rows to Skeleton Problems.

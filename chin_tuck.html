Conventions

Each sample pauses on its frame with the crop box drawn.
1/2/3 = tucked / level / in the air — saves & advances.
←/→ = peek at neighboring frames (box goes dashed).
R = re-seek to the sampled frame.
U = clear this sample's saved label.
S then 1/2/3 = skip (occluded / unclear / box misses chin).
Esc = cancel the skip menu.

Judge the chin at the boxed frame. Tucked = chin pulled down toward the chest. Level = jaw roughly parallel to the ground — neither tucked nor lifted. In the air = chin lifted, jaw pointing up. The yellow box is exactly what the model will see — if it misses the chin area, skip with box misses chin.
Shortcuts: 1/2/3 = tucked / level / air  |  Space = play/pause  |  ←/→ = ±1 frame  |  R = back to sample  |  Esc = cancel skip  |  U = clear label  |  S = skip  |  N = Next  |  P = Previous  |  F = First  |  G = Next unlabeled

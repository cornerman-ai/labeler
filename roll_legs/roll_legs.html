Roll legs — how it works

Each clip is one roll: from just before it starts until just after the boxer comes up, on a loop. Rate how the boxer gets under the punch: by bending the legs or by bending the upper body. The timeline shows the guard before the roll, the roll and its lowest point. Keep your scale the same throughout: Examples has one clip per rating.

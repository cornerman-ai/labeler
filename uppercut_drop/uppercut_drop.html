Uppercut hand drop — how it works

Each clip is one uppercut: from half a second before the punch until just after it lands, on a loop. Rate only the drop of the punching hand before it comes up, not the whole punch. The timeline shows the half second before the punch and the punch up to impact. Keep your scale the same throughout: Examples has one clip per rating.
